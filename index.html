Basic Rules of Ultimate Frisbee

  1. 2 teams each with 7 players on the field.
  2. The game starts with a throw-off.
  3. You cant run while holding the disc
  4. A point is scored when a someone catches the disc in the other end zone.
  5. Turnovers when a pass is incomplete, intercepted, or goes out of bounds.
  6. Non-contact sport and physical contact results in a foul.
  7. Play to 15 points
